Build 14 fixes fuel stops and long routes.

1. Fuel: Tap Select recommended. It should plan fuel for the whole route, with no leg longer than your tank.
2. Highlights: Landmarks like the restored 1929 station show as highlights, not as fuel stops. Try Oklahoma City to Amarillo and check the highlights there.
3. Long route: Plan a multi-day ride of 1,000+ miles. It should load without freezing, the mileage while loading should match the final mileage, there should be a hotel at each overnight stop, and gas should be grouped by leg and day, with "Past your range" only where it's true.
4. Stability: General fixes. Tell us if anything crashes or hangs.

Known issue: Two fuel stops a few miles apart on Oklahoma City to Amarillo. We know about this and it's logged, so no need to report it.
