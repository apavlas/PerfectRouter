Build 12 fixes gas stop planning.

1. Plan a long ride (500+ mi). Your first suggested gas stop should be within tank range of the start.
2. Look at the gaps between gas stops. None should be longer than your tank.
3. A "Past your range" warning should only appear where there's truly no station, and only on that leg's header.
4. Each stop's alternates should be close to it, not miles back near the previous stop.
5. Tell us if any stop feels too early or too late for how you actually ride.
