PerfectRouter Privacy Policy

Effective: September 16, 2026
Contact: anton@pavlas.com

PerfectRouter is a motorcycle ride-planning app. This policy describes how the app handles information.

  • Location is used on-device to plan rides and recommend stops.
  • Location is only shared with Apple Maps or Google Maps when you tap Navigate or Share.
  • Weather uses Apple WeatherKit for route rain risk.
  • Preferences (tank range, route style) stay on your device.
  • Rides are saved on your device; shared links encode the route you choose to send.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. This build does not include analytics SDKs.

For privacy questions, contact anton@pavlas.com.
